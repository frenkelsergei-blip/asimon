The stakes change

Two squares that change what a word is worth before anybody has said a thing.

Gamble

BET

A real bet. The board deals the hard words and starts the short clock — so the word is worth two more, a wrong shout costs two, and if nobody gets it at all the giver loses one. Nearly a third of them die. The only square you can go backwards on.

Two words

TWO

The giver holds two words and gets one sentence for both. Each is worth a point less, and the round runs until both are found or the clock stops. Whoever says one takes it.
