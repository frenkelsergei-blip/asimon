Cards and wildcards

What sits on top of the squares: three cards to choose between, or one roll you do not choose at all.

ASIMN
Card squaresThree cards offered, one kept, played later while the clock runs. Every board keeps them in one lane at a fixed interval — on the classic board, the second lane every third row — so you can see one coming and steer for it.
Wildcard squaresThe same idea, rarer on four boards of the five, and never in the card squares’ lane. One roll, applied where you stand and never re-examined — a leap onto another wildcard stops there.
35%A free cardOne of the seven, drawn at random, straight into your hand.
20%Leap aheadTwo rows forward, for nothing — never past the last row.
15%Slipped backOne row backwards, and never off the board.
10%Stole a pointOne point off the highest-scoring other unit and onto you — nothing, if no other unit has one yet.
10%Swapped placesYou trade squares with somebody genuinely out on the board — not one on the start line, not one already home.
10%JackpotTwo points, on the spot.
The seven cards
StopwatchSlam the clock down to 30 seconds, right now.
InsightAll four words the giver was offered go up on the screen. It is one of these.
VetoThe giver must say a brand new sentence, and may not reuse a single word from the first.
MimeThe giver must deliver the whole thing again with no words at all.
DoubleWhatever you score this round, double it.
SwitchOnly the giver may play this. Change to a different word. Anyone already locked out stays locked out.
BlindfoldThe next round turns Blind. Whoever gives next has to guess instead, while everybody else sees the word.
