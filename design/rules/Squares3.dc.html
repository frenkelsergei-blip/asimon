Partners

PAIR

The game draws the giver a partner, and the whole table sees who. Anyone may still answer — but if that partner is the one who gets it, the giver takes a point more.

The aimDrawn by the game, shown to the table, and the giver cannot change it.
Who may answerEverybody, as usual. The partner is a target, not a gate.
If the partner gets itThe giver takes 1 more — the ordinary aim bonus, on a target they did not choose.

Duel

DUEL

The giver names one person out loud, and only that person may answer. Everybody else watches. One shout, right or wrong, and the round is over.

The aimNamed out loud, and the giver’s own choice.
Who may answerThat one person. Everybody else watches.
One shout ends itRight or wrong. A miss locks out the only person who could answer, so the round is over.
If they get itThe giver takes 2 more, not 1 — there was no net.
