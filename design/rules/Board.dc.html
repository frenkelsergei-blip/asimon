Points are steps

Every point a unit scored this round is one step: one row forward, drifting at most one lane left or right per row. The first step off the start line is the exception — it may land in any of the four lanes.

You may stop on any square you can reach — you need not spend every step. You do have to spend one: a unit that scored always moves.

Every square’s name is on your phone before you commit, which is what makes the move the real decision in this game.

Before you have scored

A unit that has not scored waits on the start line, which is not a square. It rotates by round number — Standard, Partners, Fast — so the whole table is standing on the same one. Past the finish it is always Standard.

Winning

First unit past the last row takes it. The board is 20 rows for two racing, 17 for three, 16 for four or five, 14 for six, 12 for seven or more — a crowd sharing one phone lengthens it, and the mode and the map move it again. In Challenge 20 points takes it as well, whichever arrives first.
