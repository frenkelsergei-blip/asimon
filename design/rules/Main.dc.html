Every round

Ten kinds of round sit on the board. This page is what they all share — the six after it say only what changes.

ASIMN
The round, in five lines
1Four words on the giver’s phone, worth 1 to 4. The dearer the word, the harder it is.
2The giver sets the price first — the three below.
3The giver aims at one person. Silently, before the clock starts, and they must aim to start.
4One sentence, said once. Ninety seconds on the regular clock — Quick, Slow and Challenge each run their own.
5Whoever thinks they have it buzzes and says it out loud. The giver judges.

Scoring is what moves you: the points a round pays are the rows you then walk, and a round pays twice — the word to whoever got it, the timing to whoever gave it. Points picked up on a wildcard square are score alone and move nobody. A score never drops below zero.
