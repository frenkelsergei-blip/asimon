Asimon · the mark

The token, struck

ASIMN

Hebrew puts a round ס second, English puts a round O fifth, and both are replaced by the same struck coin — so the two wordmarks are one mark in two scripts, and that mark is the object the game is named after. The milled edge and the darker rim are what hold the coin against paper; the groove is inset rather than edge-to-edge, which keeps it a channel and not a prohibition sign.

Rubik 800 across both scripts. Amber is already the game’s secret-round colour, so the logo brings nothing new into the palette.
