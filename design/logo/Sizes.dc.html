Milling dropped, rim kept. Favicon, topbar, score rows.

The milling is the first thing to go — below about 40 px the notches collapse into a fuzzy ring and the coin reads dirty rather than struck. The compact cut keeps the two-tone rim, which is the part actually doing the work: it is what gives an amber disc a defined edge on a paper background.
