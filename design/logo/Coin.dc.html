ASIMN

The token as a struck coin: milled edge, inner rim, amber. This one leans on the memory — people who actually carried an asimon recognise it, and the amber is already the game’s own secret-round colour.

Tradeoff: the milling and the rim are the whole idea, and both are gone by 24px. It is also the most nostalgic reading, which may age the game rather than the name.
